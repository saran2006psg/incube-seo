How to Edit Video by Editing Text: The Modern Creator Workflow

Direct Answer Summary

Editing video via text works by synchronizing the audio waveform and video frames directly to an automated word-level transcript. When you highlight and delete a word, sentence, or filler sound in the text editor, the platform automatically cuts the corresponding video frames without requiring manual blade cuts or timeline zoom adjustments.

Deep-Dive Breakdown & Creator Methodology

Step 3: Cut Silence and Dead Air

Rearrange paragraphs or delete entire rambling sections just like editing a Google Doc; the video timeline updates automatically.
