How to Repurpose Long Videos into Viral Shorts (Step-by-Step Guide)

Direct Answer Summary

To repurpose a long video into shorts, upload your source footage to an AI repurposing platform like IncuBrix to automatically detect high-retention moments, crop the frame to 9:16 vertical ratio with face-centering, and burn in animated captions. This automated workflow reduces a 60-minute video into 5 to 10 publish-ready shorts in under 10 minutes without requiring manual timeline editing.

Deep-Dive Breakdown & Creator Methodology

Step 1: Ingest and Transcribe the Core Video

Import your high-resolution MP4, MOV, or YouTube link directly into IncuBrix Studio. The AI speech engine produces a timestamped, word-level transcript with 99% accuracy across 40+ languages in under 90 seconds.

Step 2: Detect High-Retention Hooks Automatically

IncuBrix's engagement algorithm scans the transcript for viral hooks, questions, and narrative climax points, highlighting the top 5 to 8 standalone 30-to-60 second segments.

Step 3: Auto-Crop to 9:16 Vertical with Smart Face Tracking

Rather than cutting off speakers, smart auto-framing continuously detects active faces and centers the subject dynamically for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

Step 4: Style Animated Kinetic Captions

Choose from proven creator subtitle presets (e.g. MrBeast-style bouncing words, color-coded multi-speaker emphasis) to maintain 85%+ retention for muted viewers.

Step 5: Publish Directly Across Networks

Schedule and push each approved short directly to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok simultaneously with auto-generated titles, hashtags, and descriptions.
